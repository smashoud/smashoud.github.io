ShiftNote – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

ShiftNote is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to ShiftNote is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not run a server for ShiftNote, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your information. This version of the app has no internet permission.

Information the app handles

Handover notes you write. Your workplace name, the names and roles of staff you add, shift names and times, handover entries, issues with their priority, comments and closing notes, and the names, times and optional signatures of people who acknowledge a handover. Any quick phrases you add or change.

Messages you paste or share. When you paste a message, or share one to ShiftNote from a chat app, the text is read on the phone and split into lines so you can choose which ones go into the handover you are writing. Only the lines you choose are kept, as handover entries. The message is not stored separately and is never uploaded.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups and exports. A backup or export file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose. If you set a password the backup is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you keep and share those files.

Workplace and personal information

ShiftNote is often used on a shared team phone or tablet, so everyone who can open the app on that device can read what is written in it. Follow your workplace's rules on record keeping and confidentiality. Do not record more personal or patient-identifying detail than you need: use bed or room numbers or initials instead of full names. When you share a handover as text or as an image, it goes to the app and the people you choose, and from then on it is handled under that app's own terms.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up, or when you choose to open a call, text message, email or WhatsApp chat from the app; that message is then handled by the app you chose.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising or analytics software. If a future free version shows ads, they will be supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interaction data; you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, this policy will be updated first, and the information you store in the app will never be shared with advertisers.

Security

Your information is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android cloud backup of app data is switched off for ShiftNote. No storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your information stays until you delete it. You can delete single records, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

ShiftNote is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
