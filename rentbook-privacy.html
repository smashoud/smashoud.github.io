RentBook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

RentBook is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to RentBook is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not run a server for RentBook, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your information. This version of the app has no internet permission.

Information the app handles

Tenancy records you add. Property names and addresses, the name and phone number of the other party, rent terms, deposits, payments, receipts, signatures drawn on the screen, condition reports and the photos you attach. All of it is kept on this phone. It only leaves the phone when you choose to share a receipt, report, statement or backup file yourself.

Payment messages you paste or share, and photos of receipts. When you paste a mobile money or bank message, share a message or picture to RentBook, or photograph a paper receipt or bank slip, the app reads the amount, date, payment method and reference on your phone to fill in the payment form. The message text and the picture are not kept after the form is filled and are never uploaded.

Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Gallery access is limited to the image you pick. Photos stay on your phone.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups and exports. A backup or export file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose. If you set a password the backup is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you keep and share those files.

Other people's details

You may enter the name, phone number and signature of your landlord or tenant. You are responsible for having their agreement to keep these details and for sharing them only with people who should see them.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for a condition report or to read a receipt or bank slip. Condition report photos are stored inside the app on this phone. A photo taken to read a receipt is used once to fill in the form and is not kept.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up, or when you choose to open a call, text message, email or WhatsApp chat from the app; that message is then handled by the app you chose.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising or analytics software. If a future free version shows ads, they will be supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interaction data; you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, this policy will be updated first, and the information you store in the app will never be shared with advertisers.

Security

Your information is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android cloud backup of app data is switched off for RentBook. No storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your information stays until you delete it. You can delete single records, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

RentBook is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
