TermWise – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

TermWise is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to TermWise is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not run a server for TermWise, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your information. This version of the app has no internet permission.

Information the app handles

School costs you add. Your children's first names or nicknames, school and class, school year and term dates, the costs you enter (category, description, amount, due date), the payments you record (amount, date, method and any receipt photo you attach) and your savings pot entries. The app does not connect to any school, bank or mobile money account and never asks for card numbers or passwords.

Text you paste or share, and photos of bills. When you paste a fee notice or payment message, share a message or picture to TermWise, or photograph a school bill or receipt, the app reads the amounts, dates, names and reference on your phone to fill in a form for you to check. The text is not kept after the form is filled, a photo is kept only if you save it with a payment as its receipt, and neither is ever uploaded.

Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Gallery access is limited to the image you pick. Photos stay on your phone.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups and exports. A backup or export file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose. If you set a password the backup is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you keep and share those files.

Children's information

TermWise is used by parents and guardians. Anything you enter about a child stays on your phone unless you choose to share a statement image or export a file yourself.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up, or when you choose to open a call, text message, email or WhatsApp chat from the app; that message is then handled by the app you chose.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising or analytics software. If a future free version shows ads, they will be supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interaction data; you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, this policy will be updated first, and the information you store in the app will never be shared with advertisers.

Security

Your information is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android cloud backup of app data is switched off for TermWise. No storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your information stays until you delete it. You can delete single records, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

TermWise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
