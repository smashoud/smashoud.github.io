What CareDose does

CareDose keeps the list of medicines and times you enter, reminds you on this phone when a dose is due and keeps a record of the doses you mark as confirmed, skipped or missed. It is a personal organiser. It is not a legal, financial, medical or official record-keeping service.

Reminders

We work to make reminders reliable, but delivery depends on your phone's settings, battery saving features and notification permission. Reminders can be delayed by several minutes or missed. Do not rely on CareDose as your only reminder for anything important.

Not a medical device and not medical advice

CareDose is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, does not give medical advice and never suggests, calculates or checks a dose, a medicine or a schedule. Everything shown comes from what you typed or chose. Check it against the label and the prescription.

The quick picks for form, how often and instructions, and the clock times they fill in, are common wordings offered to save typing. They are not a recommendation. CareDose contains no list of medicines or strengths. Words read from a photo of a label can be wrong or incomplete, so check every word against the label itself before saving. The charts and the refill profile only count what was marked in the app and are estimates.

Always follow the instructions of your doctor, pharmacist or nurse. If a dose is missed, taken twice or taken late, or you are unsure what to do, ask a health professional. In an emergency call your local emergency number.

Reminders can arrive late or not at all, and a dose marked as confirmed only records that someone tapped the button. Do not rely on CareDose alone where a missed dose could cause harm. Stock counts and adherence percentages are estimates based on your entries.
