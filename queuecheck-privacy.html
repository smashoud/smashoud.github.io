QueueCheck – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

QueueCheck is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to QueueCheck is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not run a server for QueueCheck, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your information. This version of the app has no internet permission.

Information the app handles

Places and waits you add. The names, types, area notes and opening hours of places you enter; the waits you log (arrival day and time, minutes waited and, if you enter it, the number of people ahead of you); visits you plan; and the optional name you choose to put on reports you share. Reports you paste in. When you paste a report code that someone sent you, the place name, time, wait and sender name inside that code are saved on your phone. Text you paste or share in. When you paste a message, tap From clipboard, or choose Share and then QueueCheck in another app, that text (a report message or a queue ticket SMS) is read on the phone to fill in a form for you to check, and is never uploaded. Only what you confirm is saved: for a queue ticket that is the ticket number, people ahead and the estimated wait. The app does not use your location and cannot read your messages by itself; it only sees text you paste or share into it. What you came for. If you choose a purpose for a visit (for example renewal, payment or consultation), it is saved with that visit.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups and exports. A backup or export file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose. If you set a password the backup is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you keep and share those files.

Report codes

When you tap Share, QueueCheck builds a message containing a short code. The code holds only that report: place name, place type, area note, arrival time, minutes waited, people ahead, the purpose of the visit if you chose one, and the name you chose to show, if any. It is not encrypted, so anyone who receives the message can read it. QueueCheck does not send anything by itself; your phone's share screen opens and you choose the app and the person.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up, or when you choose to open a call, text message, email or WhatsApp chat from the app; that message is then handled by the app you chose.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising or analytics software. If a future free version shows ads, they will be supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interaction data; you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, this policy will be updated first, and the information you store in the app will never be shared with advertisers.

Security

Your information is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android cloud backup of app data is switched off for QueueCheck. No storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your information stays until you delete it. You can delete single records, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

QueueCheck is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
