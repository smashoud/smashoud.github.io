Slotbook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Slotbook is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to Slotbook is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not run a server for Slotbook, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your information. This version of the app has no internet permission.

Information the app handles

Your appointment book. Your business name, phone number and the payment details text you type in; your services, prices and working hours; your clients' names, phone numbers and notes; and your appointments, including status, deposits and notes. You are responsible for having your clients' agreement to keep their contact details. The app never asks for card numbers or banking passwords.

Messages you paste or share. When you paste a client's chat message or a mobile money SMS, or share one to Slotbook from another app, the text is read on your phone to fill in a booking form or a deposit check for you to confirm. Only what you then save is kept (for example the client's name and number, the deposit amount and its reference); the message itself is not stored and is never uploaded.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups and exports. A backup or export file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose. If you set a password the backup is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you keep and share those files.

Messages to clients. Slotbook does not send anything by itself. When you tap WhatsApp, SMS or Share, the app hands the prepared text and, where needed, the client's phone number to the app you chose, and you decide whether to send it. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up, or when you choose to open a call, text message, email or WhatsApp chat from the app; that message is then handled by the app you chose.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising or analytics software. If a future free version shows ads, they will be supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interaction data; you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, this policy will be updated first, and the information you store in the app will never be shared with advertisers.

Security

Your information is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android cloud backup of app data is switched off for Slotbook. No storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your information stays until you delete it. You can delete single records, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

Slotbook is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
