Kept – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Kept is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to Kept (photos of receipts and documents, names, prices, dates and notes) is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not have a server for Kept, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your items.

Information the app handles

Items you add. Photos, text you type and text read from your photos. Reading text from photos happens on your phone; photos are not sent anywhere for this.

Pasted or shared text and pictures. If you paste the text of an e-receipt email or SMS, or share text or a picture to Kept from another app, it is read on your phone to fill in the shop, price and date for you to check. It is never uploaded. The text is kept with the item, on your phone, only if you save the item. The clipboard is read only when you tap From clipboard.

Insights. The charts and totals are worked out on your phone from the items you have saved. Nothing is sent anywhere to produce them.

Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Gallery access is limited to the image you pick.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups. A backup file is created only when you ask for one, and is saved where you choose. If you set a password it is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you store and share that file.

Advertising (free version)

Versions of Kept that show ads use Google AdMob. In those versions Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. Your receipts, documents and photos are never shared with advertisers. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Versions without ads do not include any advertising or analytics software.

Sharing

We do not sell or share the items you store. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up.

Security

Items are kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android's cloud backup of app data is switched off for Kept, so your items are not copied to a cloud account automatically. No method of storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your items.

Keeping and deleting data

Your items stay until you delete them. You can delete single items, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

Kept is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
