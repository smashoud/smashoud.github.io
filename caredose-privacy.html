CareDose – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

CareDose is published by GalamseyGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Everything you add to CareDose is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. We do not run a server for CareDose, you do not create an account, and we cannot see your information. This version of the app has no internet permission.

Information the app handles

People and medicines you add. The names, phone numbers, relationships and notes you type for the people you care for (or for yourself and your carer), the medicines you enter (name, strength, form, instructions, times, dates, stock count and any photo of the pack) and the record of each dose you mark as confirmed or skipped, including the reason you type. This is health information. It is kept only in the app's private storage on this phone. CareDose does not connect to any pharmacy, clinic, insurer or health record.

Photos of a label or pack. When you photograph a pharmacy label or a box, or share a photo to CareDose from another app, the words in the photo are read on this phone and listed next to the form so that you can copy a line into it. The photo is kept as that medicine's pack photo until you remove it. The photo and the words read from it are never uploaded, and CareDose fills in nothing from them by itself.

Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Gallery access is limited to the image you pick. Photos stay on your phone.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content leaves the device.

Backups and exports. A backup or export file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose. If you set a password the backup is encrypted with AES-256. You are responsible for where you keep and share those files.

Health information

CareDose is not connected to another phone. The carer and the person taking the medicine do not share data through the app. A message, daily summary, weekly report or CSV file leaves the phone only when you choose to send it, through the app you pick (for example WhatsApp, text message or email), and you decide who receives it. Only enter information about another person if you are caring for them or have their agreement.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Information leaves the app only when you use Share, Export or Back up, or when you choose to open a call, text message, email or WhatsApp chat from the app; that message is then handled by the app you chose.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising or analytics software. If a future free version shows ads, they will be supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interaction data; you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, this policy will be updated first, and the information you store in the app will never be shared with advertisers.

Security

Your information is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. You can turn on App lock to require your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android cloud backup of app data is switched off for CareDose. No storage is perfectly secure; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can see your information.

Keeping and deleting data

Your information stays until you delete it. You can delete single records, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.

Children

CareDose is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
