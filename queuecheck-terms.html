What QueueCheck does

QueueCheck keeps a log of how long you waited at clinics, banks and offices, works out the quieter times from that log, and reminds you about visits you plan. It is a personal organiser. It is not a legal, financial, medical or official record-keeping service.

Reminders

We work to make reminders reliable, but delivery depends on your phone's settings, battery saving features and notification permission. Reminders can be delayed by several minutes or missed. Do not rely on QueueCheck as your only reminder for anything important.

Estimates only

QueueCheck has no live information about any queue. Averages, quiet hours, best times and times to avoid are worked out only from waits you logged and reports you pasted in, and they can be wrong, out of date or based on very few reports. Opening hours are whatever you typed; hours filled in from the built-in list are only typical ones and may not match your place. Numbers read from a queue ticket message are shown for you to check and may be read wrongly. Always check with the place itself before an important or urgent visit. QueueCheck is not connected to, or endorsed by, any clinic, bank, office or other place you add. In a medical emergency go to the nearest emergency service; do not wait for a quiet time.
